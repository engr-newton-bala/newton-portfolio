Executive Profile

Precision Planning & Operations Excellence

I am Newton Bala, a seasoned Planning & Supply Chain Management professional with 7.5+ years of comprehensive industrial experience spanning both Knit and Woven apparel manufacturing. Currently, I serve as Assistant Manager in Central Planning at NZ Group.

My core strength lies in translating complex buyer orders into streamlined, lean manufacturing plans. From Yarn in-house schedules to cutting, sewing, and final shipment, I orchestrate end-to-end Time & Action (TNA) calendars, capacity planning, and micro production plans (MPP) with high data integrity.

With an engineering degree from DUET, continuous professional certifications in TQM, PMTS, and KPI management, and hands-on expertise in ERP workflows (Logic, Pridesys), I bridge floor-level industrial engineering with enterprise supply chain execution.

Core Pillars of Execution
