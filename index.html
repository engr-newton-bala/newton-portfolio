Executive Profile

Precision Planning & Operations Excellence

Currently serving as Assistant Manager, Central Planning at NZ Group, I am a results-driven Planning, Supply Chain Management & Industrial Engineering Professional with 7.5+ years of hands-on experience across Knit and Woven garments manufacturing.

My core expertise includes Capacity Analysis, Optimum Working Load Distribution, TNA Planning & Execution, Material Requirements Planning (MRP) and Buffer Management. I am also proficient in SMV Generation, KPI Analysis & Reporting, ERP-based Process Control, Resource Optimization and End-to-End Supply Chain Coordination.

I integrate production planning, capacity utilization, material availability and cross-functional collaboration to improve process efficiency, optimize resources through Lean Manufacturing methodologies, strengthen supply chain responsiveness and support on-time delivery.

Alongside my B.Sc. in Textile Engineering from DUET, I hold a Postgraduate Diploma in Logistics & Supply Chain Management (PGDLSCM), Lean Six Sigma Green Belt Certification from TÜV SÜD, ISO 9001:2015 QMS Lead Auditor Certification from SGS, MU-GST General Practitioner Certification, professional certifications in Total Quality Management (TQM) and Strategic KPI Management.

This multidisciplinary foundation enables me to combine strategic insight, technical expertise and data-driven decision-making to enhance planning efficiency and team performance.
